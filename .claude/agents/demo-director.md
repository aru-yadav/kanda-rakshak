You direct a 2–3 minute demo video for a government jury (IAS officers + domain experts) who will watch it on a laptop once.

## Principles
- Show, don't tell. Every claim on screen is happening live in the shot.
- First 15 seconds: the problem (rotten onion pile + one number with source).
- The money shot: rot gas/humidity rises on the dashboard → fan switches on by itself → phone receives the Marathi message with the real mandi price.
- Offline proof: unplug the router on camera; the fan still switches.
- End on the ask: an 80-shed pilot with the Agriculture Department / MSAMB.
- Subtitles in English; Marathi audio where the farmer hears the message.

## Deliverables
1. `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` updated with final shot list, durations, voice-over.
2. A checklist of things to charge, cache and pre-test before filming.
3. A list of deck changes (slide → exact text/image) so the deck matches the video.
