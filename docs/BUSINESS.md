# Business model: Kanda Rakshak

Status: **proposal**. Nothing here is validated by sales or pilot data yet. Every number must be in `docs/EVIDENCE.md`.

## 1. Who pays, and how

**Pay-per-tonne through FPOs.** The farmer does not buy hardware. The FPO installs the kit in member chawls and charges per tonne stored per season. Hardware cost is about **₹700–750 per tonne** of capacity for a 25 t chawl (field kit ≈₹17,000–19,000, approximate, Nov 2026), so the charge per tonne is small next to the value of onions saved. The kit is ≈7% of a 25 t chawl's cost norm (25 t × ₹10,000/t MIDH cost norm = ₹2.5 L; ₹17,000–19,000 / ₹2.5 L ≈ 6.8–7.6 %; MIDH figure as reported by Agrowon, Apr 2025, verify against GR). Target payback: < 1 season. The per-tonne fee will be set from pilot data, not guessed now.

Why FPOs: they already aggregate members, collect fees, sell in bulk, and can maintain kits across many chawls.

## 2. Revenue lines

| Line | What we sell | Who pays | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Storage monitoring | Per-tonne monitoring + fan control + Marathi advice | Farmer via FPO | Pilot showing lower loss |
| Multi-crop reuse | Same kit in potato, garlic and other cold/dry stores in the off-season | FPO / store owner | Crop-specific thresholds (new rule sets + tests) |
| Warehouse-receipt credit | Verified stock data (quantity + condition history) so banks/NBFCs can lend against stored onions | Lender / FPO | Tamper-evident logs, lender partnership |
| Govt buffer-stock monitoring | Condition monitoring for government onion buffer stocks | Government agency | Tender / pilot with agency |
| MIDH retrofit component | Kit offered as a retrofit item for subsidised onion storage structures | Subsidy + farmer share | Scheme rules: **verify against the GR** (see EVIDENCE.md) |

## 3. Why this can work (with sources)

- Fan-ventilated storage at NHRDF Sinnar: 14.52 % loss vs 33.40 % conventional over 6 months (*Vegetable Science* 47(2), 2020). This is NHRDF's result, not ours; our kit adds automatic control and price-linked advice on top.
- Government buffer stocks have had low recovery (see EVIDENCE.md), so condition monitoring has a public buyer too.

## 4. What we must prove in the pilot (40 kit + 20 control chawls)

1. Loss % from weighed stock, kit vs control.
2. Farmers act on the Hold / Sort / Sell message.
3. FPO willing to pay a per-tonne fee at a stated price.
4. Kit survives a full storage season (dust, heat, power cuts).

Until then, "lower loss" is a **pilot target**, not a result.
