Price shown is an example (₹2,400/q, Lasalgaon). The real kit pulls the daily modal price from Govt Agmarknet data on data.gov.in.

1 · Install

3 sensors on a probe stick in the heap, gas pod on top, outside sensor, fan, solar box. No new building.

2 · Watch

Heat, humidity and rot gas measured at top, middle and bottom every 15 minutes.

3 · Act

Fan switches on by itself when the heap is humid and outside air is drier. Works with no network.

4 · Tell

One Marathi instruction on any phone: hold, check, sort or sell, with today's mandi price.
