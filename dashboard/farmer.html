Farmer's view

This is what a farmer with a smartphone sees. Farmers with a basic keypad phone get the same instruction as a Marathi voice call and SMS. No app, reading or internet needed.

  • One status word: सुरक्षित (safe) / लक्ष ठेवा (watch) / धोका (danger)
  • One instruction: hold, check, sort or sell
  • Today's mandi price from Govt Agmarknet data, with date and source
  • Listen button: the same message as Marathi voice

Demo: data comes from the Kanda Rakshak digital twin (simulated sheds) until the hardware is installed.
